# AI Strategy Pack — Australian Banking

Materials for a strategic conversation on AI, operating models, and comparative advantage in a large Australian bank / financial-services organisation. Core thesis: **advantage comes from how fast the organisation metabolises AI — not from tool access.**

## Contents

## The three numbers that anchor the argument

1. **95% / 5%** — MIT: 95% of GenAI pilots deliver no P&L impact; the 5% that work embed AI into workflows that learn.
2. **10-20-70** — BCG: 10% of the effort is algorithms, 20% tech and data, 70% people and process.
3. **#4** — Evident AI Index: CommBank is already the world's #4 bank for AI maturity.

Plus one regulatory anchor: **APRA's April 2026 letter to industry on AI** — the governance agenda is now explicit; the strategic choice is whether to meet it as a floor or build it into a paved road.
